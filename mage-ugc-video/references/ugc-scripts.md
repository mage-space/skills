# UGC scripts

## Timing

| Length | Spoken words | Beats |
|---|---|---|
| 10 s | 20–25 | Hook (0–2 s) · value (2–8 s) · CTA (8–10 s) |
| 15 s | 35–40 | Hook (0–2 s) · show (2–7 s) · proof or detail (7–12 s) · CTA (12–15 s) |
| 20–30 s | 50–75 | Two show beats; split into two clips if the model drifts |

People speak about 2.5 words a second on camera. Leave half a second of silence at each end.

## Hooks

- **Surprise:** "Okay, I did not expect this to actually work."
- **Callout:** "If your skin gets dry by noon, stop scrolling."
- **Confession:** "I've been gatekeeping this for months."
- **Result first:** "This is my skin after two weeks." (only if true)
- **Question:** "Why does nobody talk about this?"
- **Unboxing:** "It's here. Let's open it."

## Scripts by format

**review (15 s)**
> "Okay, I didn't expect this serum to feel this light. It sinks in in, like, seconds. And look at this dropper, no mess. Honestly? It's staying on my shelf. Link's below."

**unboxing (15 s)**
> "It's finally here! Okay, the box is gorgeous. And… oh, look at the color of that bottle. First impression: it smells amazing. Trying it tonight, I'll report back."

**how_to (15 s)**
> "Here's how I use it. Two drops, straight onto clean skin. Press it in, don't rub. Then moisturizer on top. That's it. Glow for days."

**try_on (10 s)**
> "Okay, the fit. Look at this. It's so soft, and it doesn't cling. I'm obsessed."

**routine (15 s)**
> "Morning routine, real quick. Coffee first, obviously. Then this, two drops. It makes my makeup sit so much better. Done in two minutes."

**problem_solution (15 s)**
> "My skin was so dull this winter. Nothing helped. Then I tried this. Two weeks in, and people keep asking what I changed. This."

The scripts assume the user supplied these talking points. Every product statement, sensory details included (texture, scent, absorption, fit), comes from the user's facts or product page. With no talking points, ask for two or three; never fill the gap with invented ones.

## Direction for the presenter

- Hold the product at chest height, label toward the camera, when it's first named.
- One gesture per beat: hold up, tap, open, apply, turn.
- Natural pauses, small laughs, and casual phrasing ("honestly", "okay") read as real.
- Handheld front camera, eye level, arm's length. Real rooms: bathroom counter, kitchen, bedroom, car.

## Prompt phrasing

- "UGC style, handheld smartphone footage, natural light, casual, authentic, slight camera movement."
- Quote the spoken lines in order: `They say: "…" Then: "…"`.
- End with the product lock: "Keep the product identical to @image2: shape, colors, label."
- "No music" unless the user wants a track; UGC usually runs on voice and room tone.
