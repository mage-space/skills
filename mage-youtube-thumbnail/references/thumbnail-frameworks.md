# Thumbnail frameworks

A thumbnail must open an **information gap**: the image raises a question that only the video answers. Brainstorm at least five concepts across these frameworks, then keep the strongest. Two frameworks can combine when it deepens the gap without adding clutter.

**Truthfulness:** exaggerate, but never misrepresent. A thumbnail that lies costs trust and watch time.

| # | Framework | How to render it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before / after | Two states of one subject with maximum contrast; a split layout only here or when asked. |
| 2 | Social UI | A generic chat bubble, DM, or review card beside the subject with a short true line (baked via Flare). No real platform branding. |
| 3 | Three-step progression | Three panels: start → middle → end, optional "Day 1 / Day 30" badges. |
| 4 | Real screenshot | Not a generation: suggest a real frame from the video, or a posed shot matching it. |
| 5 | Posed portrait | The default: the subject huge in frame with a strong emotion, little else. |
| 6 | Action shot | One intriguing action frozen mid-moment, uncluttered. |
| 7 | Specific day | Any framework plus a big "DAY N" badge from late in the story. |
| 8 | Graphic | A clean, familiar chart or diagram instead of a photo; drop the lighting rig. |
| 9 | Landscape | The place is the hero; one small subject on a third for scale. |
| 10 | Map or aerial | A map or aerial view with a highlighted route, circle, or arrow. |
| 11 | Product | The product as the hero and the answer to the title's question. |
| 12 | Text callout | An arrow and one word, or text that completes the title's question (baked via Flare). |
| 13 | Repetition | A huge quantity of one object filling the frame, with one subject for scale. |
| 14 | Size difference | Extreme scale contrast between two things central to the story. |
| 15 | News clip | A generic "breaking news" lower-third with a short true line; no real network branding. |
| 16 | Amplified reality | One real element of the story exaggerated, still plausible. |

## Rules that make them work

- One clear focal subject.
- A strong information gap: the viewer needs the video to resolve it.
- High contrast and legibility at about 120 px wide.
- Emotion or intrigue on every face.
- No clutter: remove anything that doesn't serve the gap.
- Readable in under a second in a crowded feed.
- Truthful to the video.

## Reading a style-reference thumbnail

Extract the structure, never the identity: the subject's pose and action, key elements, location, composition, background, whether it's split (and into how many panels), the number of people, and the emotion. Use it as art direction; the user's instructions override it field by field.
