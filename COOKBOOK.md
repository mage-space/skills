# Cookbook

Recipes that chain skills. Each line is something you can say to your agent with the Mage connector on.

## Launch a product from one photo

1. `mage-product-photoshoot`: "Shoot my serum in five styles for the launch: studio, bathroom shelf, hands applying it, a splash concept, and a Pinterest flat lay. Here's the photo: <link>."
2. `mage-marketplace-cards`: "Now make the Amazon image set from the same photo: main image, infographic, detail shot, and three A+ modules. Key facts: 15% vitamin C, hyaluronic acid, fragrance-free, vegan."
3. `mage-ugc-video`: "Make a 10-second UGC review of it: a woman in her 20s in her bathroom, casual and excited."

The product photo is the reference for everything, so the bottle looks the same in every image and the video.

## Test and scale a paid ad

1. `mage-ad-multiplier`: "Make four variants of this ad for testing: a different model, a different outfit, a rooftop setting, and the headline 'Summer in a can'. <link>"
2. `mage-ad-resizer`: "Resize the winner for 1:1, 9:16, and 16:9."
3. `mage-ad-localizer`: "Now Spanish for Spain, German, and Japanese versions of the 4:5."

## Start a faceless channel

1. `mage-faceless-video`: "Make a 10-second stickman short explaining leap years, vertical."
2. `mage-youtube-thumbnail`: "Design a thumbnail for a longer version of it, no face, graphic style."
3. Keep the same style key and narrator voice for every episode so the channel looks and sounds consistent.

## A creator with a consistent look

1. `mage-characters`: "Save this portrait as a character called Nova, and give her a warm, bright voice. <link>"
2. `mage-generate`: "@nova reading in a corner cafe; @nova hiking a misty ridge in a red rain jacket; @nova at a night market."
3. `mage-youtube-thumbnail`: "A thumbnail of @nova for 'I learned to surf in 30 days', shocked, giant wave behind her. Give me three emotions."

## Build a character and put them to work

1. `mage-character-builder`: "Build a character: a woman, long wavy red hair, green eyes, freckles, young adult, streetwear."
2. `mage-character-muse`: "Same person, but try the Soft and Sharp faces." Pick a take.
3. `mage-character-sheet`: "Make a character sheet from that one and save it as Nova."
4. `mage-scene-builder`: "A late night drive with @nova and @kai, 10 seconds."
5. `mage-lipsync`: "Make @nova say: 'Welcome back to the channel.'"

## One character through a series of shots

1. `mage-character-consistency`: "Design a courier for my series: late 20s, short silver hair, a scar through his LEFT eyebrow, a yellow messenger bag on his RIGHT shoulder. Make the master and the sheet first."
2. `mage-character-consistency`: "Now a turntable, then a 10-second rooftop scene where he walks away from the camera and turns back to the lens."
3. `mage-character-consistency`: "Same scene, final quality on Cherry 2 Pro."

Approve the master and the sheet before the turntable: every later shot copies them, and they cost a fraction of one video. The turntable is made once and reused, but its seconds bill again in every Lemon scene that uses it.

## Film a vertical drama series

1. `mage-characters`: "Save these two as Nora and Eli, and give each a voice that fits their face. <link> <link>"
2. `mage-short-drama`: "Episode 1 of a vertical drama: Nora runs a diner at night; her estranged brother Eli brings the key to their late father's house. 30 seconds, end on a cliffhanger."
3. `mage-short-drama`: "Episode 2 picks up from the key. Add a quiet piano underscore."

The cast and the diner stay saved by `@handle`, so every episode keeps the same faces, voices, and room. Approve the script and the storyboard stills before the scenes render: they cost a fraction of one scene.

## Fix and finish a still

1. `mage-refine`: "Fix the hands in this image. <link>"
2. `mage-inpaint`: "Now remove the coffee cup on the table."
3. `mage-relight`: "Give it soft, warm light from the upper left."
4. `mage-angles`: "And show me the same shot from a front-right three-quarter view."

## Re-cast a clip

1. `mage-video-character-swap`: "Replace the dancer in this clip with @nova. <link>"
2. `mage-video-editor`: "Now turn the studio into a rooftop at sunset."

## Remake a viral video

1. `mage-characters`: "Save this photo as a character called Nova. <link>"
2. `mage-video-remake`: "Here's a TikTok that went viral: trend.mp4. How was it made? Then remake it with @nova and my water bottle (<link>), same hook and pacing, my own captions."

The breakdown says which shots were filmed, generated, or built in code, so the plan only pays for what has to be generated. A dance or transition take is recast with Magejutsu; the rest is rebuilt shot by shot and cut to the original's timing.

## Costs

Every skill quotes the price in Gems before it runs anything big. Images cost tens to a couple of hundred Gems; video costs thousands, climbing with resolution and length. Draft video with Cherry Mini or Lemon, then render the final on Cherry 2 Pro.
