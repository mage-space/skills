# Scene presets

The app's nine presets, word for word. Replace every `{characterOne}` and `{characterTwo}` with the right text for each character, and change nothing else:

| Characters | `{characterOne}` | `{characterTwo}` |
|---|---|---|
| Two images | `the character from Image 1` | `the character from Image 2` |
| Saved character, then an image | `@one` | `the character from Image 1` |
| An image, then a saved character | `the character from Image 1` | `@two` |
| Two saved characters | `@one` | `@two` |

The image numbers count only the images sent: with one saved character and one image, the image is Image 1. A slot keeps its lowercase "the" even at the start of a sentence, as in the app, and `@handle's` works as a possessive.

Each preset is written as four shots with a few lines of dialogue. Every one states that both characters are adults; keep that.

## Boxing Match

```
Use {characterOne} and {characterTwo} as two adult boxing rivals. Keep both characters' facial features, outfits, body types, and identities consistent in every shot. Do not introduce extra main fighters.

Create a cinematic multi-cut boxing sequence in a packed arena with dramatic overhead lights, crowd noise, bell rings, glove impacts, heavy breathing, and tense sports-broadcast energy.

Shot 1: Wide establishing shot from above the ring as both fighters circle each other, bouncing on their feet while the crowd roars. The camera cranes down toward the ropes.
Shot 2: Ringside tracking shot following quick footwork and feints. {characterOne} throws a fast jab combination while {characterTwo} slips and counters. Add sweat spray, glove snap, and sharp motion blur.
Shot 3: Close-up exchange between rounds as both fighters lean near the ropes, exhausted but defiant. {characterOne} says, "You still standing?" {characterTwo} smirks and answers, "I'm just getting started."
Shot 4: Final dynamic burst with handheld ringside camera movement, a dramatic dodge, a clean body shot, and both fighters resetting in the center under flashing arena lights.

Style: high-energy cinematic sports drama, realistic choreography, clear readable faces, intense but non-gory action, smooth cuts, no distorted limbs.
```
